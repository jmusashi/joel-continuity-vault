# DCE Paradoxes

This directory preserves paradoxes encountered through the lived development, application, dogfooding, observation, and public traversal of Decision Continuity Engineering (DCE) and its related continuity architectures.

## Purpose

These paradoxes are preserved as continuity artifacts.

They are not automatically canonical claims, proofs, or finalized conclusions. They capture moments where observation, architecture, identity, continuity, interpretation, or lived experience reveal tensions that may become more meaningful through return.

The purpose of this directory is to preserve those observations so their trajectories remain recoverable.

## Current Status

The paradoxes begin primarily as **dogfooding observations**.

Their presence here means:

- the paradox was encountered;
- its formulation was worth preserving;
- its provenance should remain recoverable;
- it may be revisited as new traces appear.

Preservation does not automatically promote a paradox into DCE canon.

## Maturation Path

A paradox may move conceptually through:

Observed  
→ Persistent Recurrence  
→ Matured Paradox  
→ Canonical Candidate  
→ Applied Architecture or Publication

Promotion depends on recurrence, structural usefulness, and later human judgment.

## Operating Principle

A paradox does not need to be immediately resolved.

Sometimes its value lies in preserving the point where two apparently valid observations meet but have not yet been reconciled.

## Epistemic Boundary

Unless explicitly promoted elsewhere, material stored here should not be treated as established DCE canon.

## Continuity Principle

**Preserve the observation. Preserve the provenance. Allow the meaning to mature through return.*
