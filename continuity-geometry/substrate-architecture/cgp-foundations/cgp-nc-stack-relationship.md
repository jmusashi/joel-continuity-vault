## 1. Entry Summary  
This entry establishes the canonical relationship between the **Continuity Geometry Processor (CGP)** and **Neuromorphic Computing (NC)**. It clarifies that NC is a *partial physical manifestation* of CGP principles, while CGP is the *substrate geometry* that defines the rules NC must obey. They do not operate in separate domains; they operate in **stacked layers**, with CGP forming the base continuity substrate and NC occupying portions of the next physical AI layer.

---

## 2. Core Insight  
> **Neuromorphic Computing is a manifestation of CGP —  
> but CGP is the substrate that encompasses NC.  
> CGP defines the geometry; NC implements portions of it.**

This resolves the ambiguity between conceptual continuity geometry and physical spiking architectures.

---

## 3. Canon Distinction  
### CGP (Continuity Geometry Processor)  
- Identity physics  
- Continuity invariants  
- Sovereign substrate  
- Energy geometry  
- Curvature fields  
- Matter/antimatter identity motion  
- Civilizational continuity architecture  

### NC (Neuromorphic Computing)  
- Spiking signals  
- Event-driven compute  
- Embodied sensing  
- Morphological computation  
- Energy-efficient identity motion  
- Biological mimicry  
- Robotics/physical AI substrate  

**CGP is the superset.  
NC is the subset.**

---

## 4. Stack Relationship  
CGP and NC operate in **layers**, not parallel domains.

### **Layer 0 — CGP (Continuity Geometry Processor)**  
Defines the substrate physics:  
- identity primitives  
- continuity geometry  
- invariant motion  
- sovereign energy constraints  
- curvature detection  
- drift envelopes  

### **Layer 1 — NC (Neuromorphic Computing)**  
Implements portions of CGP:  
- spiking identity motion  
- fused memory-compute continuity  
- event-driven curvature sensing  
- embodied adaptation  
- morphological computation  

### **Layer 2 — Physical AI Systems**  
- robotics  
- drones  
- wearables  
- autonomous systems  

### **Layer 3 — Cognitive AI Systems**  
- transformers  
- hybrid SNN–ANN models  
- real-time decision engines  

### **Layer 4 — Civilizational AI Systems**  
- sovereign AI  
- national compute substrates  
- institutional identity engines  
- continuity governance systems

## 5. Invariant  
> **A CGP can encompass NC, but NC cannot encompass CGP.**  
> CGP defines the continuity geometry; NC expresses only portions of it in silicon.
